Solutions

A suite of solutions, built to get the most from Soil Booster.

A multi-step solution to cover all your needs.

01 The base product

Soil Booster

We turn olive pomace and other agricultural by-products, from the citrus and almond sectors among others, into Soil Booster, a soil improver that rebuilds organic matter fast, at the lowest cost per hectare.

One product, sourced from whatever by-product stream is available near your orchard, so the economics work wherever you farm.

02 Customisation

Customisation

Every soil is different. We analyse your samples, identify deficiencies, and mix in micronutrients and corrective elements, so the product is optimally tailored to your land conditions, for the highest impact.

No fixed recipe. What goes into your Soil Booster is set by what your soil needs.

03 Application

Application

Specialised equipment applies Soil Booster directly to the root zone, maximising efficiency and minimising soil disturbance, at the lowest application cost per hectare.

Our teams handle it from delivery to spreading, so it's one less thing on your side to manage.

04 Reporting

Reporting

Edaflow tracks every tonne in and out, and the energy used to process it. You get a detailed life-cycle analysis for each product, and over time, a trace of your soil carbon levels.

That same data supports your ESG reporting, giving your auditor a documented, product-level basis for the impact Soil Booster has on your operation. (Exact reporting requirements vary by country and by your reporting obligations, worth checking with your auditor directly.)

05 Specialty products

Specialty products

Beyond the base product, we create special mixtures of Soil Booster and seeds, built as erosion barrier products that take root swiftly.

Locally produced and built for fast establishment, these let you battle erosion in exactly the areas of your farm where it's happening, and help off-takers restore land, for instance areas affected by forest fires.

Every site is different. Tell us your soil and volumes, and we'll scope what Soil Booster could do.
