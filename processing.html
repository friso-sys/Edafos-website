O — processing

Inside a bay: how by-product becomes Soil Booster.

Every batch runs the same engineered process — feedstock in, Soil Booster out — built to run at volumes a small operator can't replicate.

A — bay schematic

Conceptual process schematic, not a rendering of a specific site. IoT sensors monitor moisture, oxygen and temperature through active composting and maturation, before screening produces finished Soil Booster.

B — the process

From feedstock to finished product.

01

Feedstock intake

Olive pomace, almond hulls and similar by-product arrive from co-located mills, minimising transport cost and emissions.

02

Active composting

Windrows are aerated and turned under IoT-guided control — moisture, oxygen and temperature held in range automatically.

03

Maturation

Material stabilises and cures, developing the organic matter structure that gives Soil Booster its soil-building effect.

04

Screening & output

Finished Soil Booster is screened for consistency, then bagged or supplied in bulk to growers and site partners.

C — why it works

Automation is what makes the volume possible.
