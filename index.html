Improve your soil, improve your margins.

Edafos operates large-scale circular processing sites. Through our Soil Booster, you rebuild soil organic matter fast, at the lowest cost possible.

  • A stable-priced source for part of your fertiliser needs, decoupled from fossil nitrogen prices
  • Reduced disposal costs through sites close to you
  • Higher yields and better crop resilience from improved soil health
  • Higher soil organic matter increases your land's asset value

Our proprietary platform

Edaflow

Processing large volumes across multiple sites takes advanced monitoring, process control, and quality and LCA reporting. It keeps cost per unit low, and nutrient retention high. That's why we built Edaflow: proprietary hardware and software, proven on our own sites first, then licensable across the basin.
